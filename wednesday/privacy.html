Wednesday privacy policy

Wednesday is used only by its owner, on their own computer. When signed in with Google it can:

  • read Gmail messages,
  • save drafts and send email, only after the owner approves each one,
  • read Google Calendar events.

This data is handled by AI models running on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The Google sign-in is stored only on that computer and can be removed anytime in Wednesday's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
